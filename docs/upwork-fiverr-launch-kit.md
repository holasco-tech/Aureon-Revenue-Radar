# Upwork and Fiverr launch kit — Aureon Revenue Radar

> Draft marketing material. Do not publish claims that a production-ready SaaS, active n8n integration, or automatic customer messaging is live. Demonstrate only tested features.

## Positioning
I help service businesses organize incoming enquiries, identify high-priority sales opportunities, and create reviewable follow-up workflows using a custom dashboard and n8n integrations.

## Fiverr gig draft
**Title:** I will build a lead tracking dashboard and n8n sales automation for your business

**Description:** Losing track of enquiries in forms, spreadsheets or CRMs? I can configure a lead-prioritization dashboard and design n8n workflows that fit your sales process. We start with a short discovery of your lead sources and approval rules. Depending on the package, deliverables may include a lead intake connection, scoring rules, owner review dashboard, testing and handover. Customer-facing messages are never enabled without your approval. Contact me before ordering so we can confirm scope and compatibility.

**Suggested starting packages (test demand before finalizing):**
- Basic $199 fixed: one intake source, demo configuration, dashboard walkthrough.
- Standard $499 fixed: up to three compatible intake sources, custom scoring, workflow test and handover.
- Premium: custom quote for CRM/API integrations, deployment and support.
- Optional hosted support: $29–59/month subject to infrastructure and agreed support scope. Subscription terms must be arranged through supported platform/payment mechanisms; do not ask marketplace clients to move payment off-platform.

**Buyer requirements:** Industry, existing lead sources, approximate lead volume, CRM used, desired follow-up actions, business rules, whether hosting is required, and data/privacy constraints.

## Upwork profile/portfolio project
**Project title:** Aureon Revenue Radar — Lead Prioritization and Sales Review Dashboard

**Short summary:** Developed a Cloudflare Workers and D1-backed lead-recovery prototype with lead import, scoring, outcome tracking, owner authentication and grounded analysis. Building a secure webhook intake and human review queue for n8n integration. Current public demo uses fictional data; production multi-tenant SaaS and live n8n delivery are not yet launched.

**Proposal opener:** Hi, I can help turn your scattered lead sources into a prioritized, reviewable workflow. I would start by mapping your lead sources and decision rules, then build a small tested integration and dashboard before expanding automation. Which CRM or form currently receives your enquiries, and what follow-up should require human approval?

## Sales sequence
1. Publish a fictional-data demo and capture a 45–90 second walkthrough.
2. Add demo to portfolio and platform project galleries; disclose prototype status.
3. Offer scoped client implementations, not an unverified plug-and-play SaaS.
4. Use marketplace-compliant contracts and payments.
5. Collect feedback from pilot clients before introducing recurring hosted subscriptions.

## Evidence required before publishing claims
- Public verified demo URL
- Screenshots or short video of real tested flow
- Signed webhook end-to-end test
- Confirmed hosting and support costs
- Data security and privacy checks
